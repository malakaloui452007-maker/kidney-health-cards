Meet Your Kidneys

Your kidneys are two bean-shaped organs located near the middle of your back, just below your rib cage.

  • They filter waste and extra water from your blood.
  • They help balance salts and minerals.
  • They help control blood pressure and support red blood cell production.
Did you know? Healthy kidneys filter your blood continuously, helping keep your body in balance.
